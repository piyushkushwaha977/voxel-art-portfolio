Can you make something that isn't on the shelf?

Yes, most orders are custom. Send a picture of the character or object, the height you want and the finish. If you already have an STL or 3MF file, even better.

What's the difference between raw, primed and hand-painted?

Raw comes straight off the printer in one colour of plastic, with faint layer lines. Primed is sanded and sprayed in one matte tone, which shows off the shape. Hand-painted is primed, then painted in full colour and sealed, so it takes the longest.

How long does an order take?

Keychains take 2 to 3 days. Most figures, lamps and frames take 7 to 14 days, depending on size and paint. You get a ready date before anything is printed.

Can I get a piece in a different size?

Most pieces scale from about 8 cm to 40 cm. Bigger prints use more material and take longer, so the price changes with the size.

How do I pay and get my order?

We sort out payment and delivery in the same chat once you've approved the details. You can collect it, or I'll pack it and send it by courier.

How strong are the prints?

They're made for display and light handling. Keep them out of direct sun and away from heat, because PLA starts to soften at around 55 °C. Thin parts like blades and wing tips are the most delicate.
